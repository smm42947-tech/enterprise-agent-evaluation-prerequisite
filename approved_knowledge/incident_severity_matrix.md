# Incident Severity Matrix

**Document ID:** KB-INC-001

A Sev1 incident is a critical incident causing major business impact, such as complete loss of a critical customer service. The support team must acknowledge it within 5 minutes, establish an incident bridge within 10 minutes, and provide stakeholder updates every 30 minutes until service is stable.

A Sev2 incident causes high but limited business impact. It must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and updated hourly.
