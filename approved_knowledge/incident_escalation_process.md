# Incident Escalation Process

**Document ID:** KB-INC-002

The incident commander performs the initial notification, starts the technical escalation path, coordinates stakeholder communication, and records the closure. For Sev1, notify the service owner and major incident manager immediately. Engage the application and infrastructure on-call teams. Publish updates every 30 minutes. After recovery, record the resolution, business impact, timeline, owners, and follow-up actions before closure.
